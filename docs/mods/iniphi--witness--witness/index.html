witness

A Claude Code mod in iniphi/witness

Keeps a ledger of what you asked for and counts it done only on evidence: claims stay pale until a read-back confirms them, bare SQL UPDATE or DELETE writes are held for your answer, and a print of what was and was not witnessed goes into the session log.

witness reaches L2 (writes or runs) and passed claude plugin validate with warnings.

  • Access from classified calls: L2, writes or runs: drives Claude, persists state, draws
