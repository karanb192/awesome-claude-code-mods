clawd-sidekick

A Claude Code mod in Tunai-0511/clawd-sidekick

Clawd's pixel world above your prompt in the Claude desktop app, in five scenes (open space among them, where everyone floats) that follow your local time, season and holidays: Clawd walks to the zone of whatever Claude is doing while his crew plays, and acts out what happens: tests, commits, pushes, permission asks, a library that fills with the context. The crew keeps its own day, rare sights turn up, and Clawds from your other sessions drop by. Name them and make them yours. A git safety net and a list of what each session changed. A Clawd spinner, 78 trophies with hats and pals to earn, a card of your day to share, live usage figures and a deadline radar. Costs no tokens. English and 繁體中文.
