vome-health

A Claude Code mod in Vortitron/home-assistant-mcp

A side pane for your Home Assistant's health: Vome's score out of 100 and what its check found, by severity with what to do, marked as Claude works on each, then a re-check rolls the score to the new one. Needs @vortitron/home-assistant-mcp with vome_health_report and the Vome integration.
