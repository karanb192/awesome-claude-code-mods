life-coach

A Claude Code mod in zwbao/claude-code-life-coach

A life coach that lives in Claude Code: notices your work rhythm, nudges at natural pauses, keeps small commitments, and coaches with Claude. Global evidence library plus a China pack. All data stays on your machine.
