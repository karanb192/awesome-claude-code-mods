kit-pet

A Claude Code mod in KitTran1307/kit-claude-mods

A small original pixel pet in a band above the prompt: it reacts to what Claude does (working, done, error, low quota), grows as you finish turns, and cheers your first milestones. Three pets, English and Vietnamese, no setup.
