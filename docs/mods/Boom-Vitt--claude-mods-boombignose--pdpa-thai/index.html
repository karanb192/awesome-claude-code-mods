pdpa-thai

A Claude Code mod in Boom-Vitt/claude-mods-boombignose

Helps reduce personal data sent to Claude: redacts detected Thai and international personal data before sending and masks it on screen. Detection runs locally and the mod makes no network calls of its own; the redacted conversation is still sent to your configured model provider (Anthropic by default). Best effort, not legal advice.
