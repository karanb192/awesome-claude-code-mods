kit-graphite

A Claude Code mod in KitTran1307/kit-claude-mods

A graphite theme for the Claude desktop and mobile apps: neutral charcoal grays with a black accent (ivory in dark mode), a field of dots frozen mid-wave behind the conversation with small motes drifting through it, and a small clay-colored pixel comet circling the middle of the screen.

kit-graphite reaches L0 (draws and remembers) by its classified calls and passed claude plugin validate with warnings.
