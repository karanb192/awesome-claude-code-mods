pixel-room

A Claude Code mod in Samarth2001/claude-plugins

A late-night pixel room behind the desktop app: the day's weather and the moon at the window, a lamp that lights at dusk and a cat you can pet that sleeps, paces and watches while Claude works, with notes for its todo list, moths for its subagents and a room card in the same voice
