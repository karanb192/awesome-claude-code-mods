jarvis

A Claude Code mod in rotembab/jarvis-claude-mod

A JARVIS-style voice for Claude Code: say "Hey Jarvis" (or hold push-to-talk) to speak, hear the reply, talk over it to interrupt. Local wake word and speech-to-text, Fish Audio speech, optional webcam hand control for the mouse and windows, control of your TVs and smart home devices, and PC control: desktop actions such as opening apps and the media keys, with a guard that asks before Claude's risky commands.
