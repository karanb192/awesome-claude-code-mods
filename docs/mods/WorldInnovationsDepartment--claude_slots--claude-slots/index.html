mind-the-mug

A Claude Code mod in WorldInnovationsDepartment/claude_slots

A kind reminder for Claude Code: don't delegate your reasoning, or you'll get a mug. Once a day, after the last response of a turn, a small pixel-art cat shows up above the prompt and knocks something off its shelf. It stays until your next prompt.

mind-the-mug reaches L2 (writes or runs) and passed claude plugin validate.
